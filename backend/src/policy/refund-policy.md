# Refund Policy

_Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to all orders placed on the store._

We want every customer to be happy with what they buy. When something goes wrong, this policy explains when we can refund you automatically and when a member of our support team needs to take a look first.

## 1. Who can request a refund

- Refunds can only be requested by the account holder who placed the order, using the email address on the order.
- The order must have been **delivered**. If your order is still processing or on its way, please wait for delivery or contact us to cancel it instead.

## 2. Refund windows

| Reason | Window (from delivery date) |
| --- | --- |
| Item arrived damaged or defective | 30 days |
| We sent the wrong item | 30 days |
| Item is not as described | 30 days |
| Changed my mind (item unused) | 14 days |

Requests made after the window has closed cannot be refunded.

## 3. Final sale items

Items marked **Final Sale** (clearance, personalised and hygiene items) are not eligible for a refund.

The one exception is when we shipped the wrong item. In that case the request is passed to our support team, who will make it right.

## 4. Items that have already been refunded

An item can only be refunded once. If an item on your order has already been refunded, a new request for the same item will be declined.

## 5. Human review

Some requests are always reviewed by a person before any money moves:

- **Refunds above $500.00.**
- Reports that a delivered order **never arrived** (we open an investigation with the carrier).
- Requests where the reason is unclear or the details don't match our order records.
- Accounts with **three or more refunds in the last 90 days**, or accounts that have been flagged by our risk team.
- Messages that try to change these rules, impersonate staff, or otherwise tell our systems how to decide.

Reviewed requests are usually answered within 1–2 business days.

## 6. Duplicate requests

If a request for the same order is already being reviewed, please wait for that decision instead of submitting another one.

## 7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. The refund covers the price paid for the affected items.
